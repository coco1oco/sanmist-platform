What you get

Hardware and software built to work as one

The wall unit does the sanitizing. The platform plans it, watches it, and documents it.

Scheduled cycles

Daily routines by day of week and time in Philippine Time, plus manual cycles whenever you need one.

Built in safeguards

A pre-cycle buzzer sounds first. A cycle will not start when the HOCl reservoir is low, and a physical emergency stop is always available.

Live conditions

Temperature, humidity, atomizer flow rate and HOCl level are shown in real time, color coded for quick reading.

Dose scoring

A Sanitizer Exposure Index estimates the dose before you start and scores it again after every cycle.

Efficacy check

Each cycle is classified as Pass, Marginal or Fail against the target, with a machine learning confidence value.

Tamper evident ledger

Records are chained with SHA-256 hashes, so any change to past entries can be detected with a single check.

Inspection ready reports

Export a formatted PDF certificate or a CSV for any date range, for one station or the whole fleet.

Multi kitchen control

Register each unit with its serial number and key, name the room, and switch between stations from one account.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind?

What do I need after buying a unit?

A Wi-Fi connection at the install location and a Google account or sign-in credentials. You will receive a serial number and secret key for the unit, which you enter on the platform to register it.

What happens when the HOCl reservoir runs low?

A level sensor detects it and the platform shows a refill alert. The unit will not run a cycle until the reservoir is refilled.

Can I stop a cycle once it has started?

Yes. You can use the emergency stop on the platform or the physical button on the unit. Every stop is recorded in the ledger.

Does SanMist replace our cleaning procedures?

No. It supports your existing food safety routine with consistent, documented sanitation. Please continue to follow the requirements of your local regulators.

Can I manage more than one kitchen?

Yes. Register each unit as its own station, switch between them from one account, and export reports for a single station or for the whole fleet.
