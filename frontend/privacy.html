SANMIST

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

SanMist is an IoT-enabled automated dry-fog sanitization monitoring and compliance logging platform developed for commercial food-service operations and academic evaluation.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or an administrator account, SanMist receives only your basic Google profile identifier, display name, and email address to create and authenticate your operator session.

2. How Information Is Used

Your account information is used solely to authenticate dashboard access, link sanitization stations to your account, and record which operator triggered or audited a sanitization cycle.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Session tokens, linked devices, schedules, and operational telemetry records are stored in the application's audit database. SanMist does not sell, rent, or share user profile information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Account & Data Removal

You may sign out at any time to invalidate your active session token, unpair linked devices from Settings, or contact the project administrator to remove your account record.
